Enable Camera Flashlight

To control your phone's LED torch, mobile browsers require one-time camera permission. No video is recorded or transmitted.

Touch & hold anywhere on the button to turn ON. Release to turn OFF.

Device & Flashlight Info

How It Works

This web app controls your mobile device's camera LED torch via the standard MediaStreamTrack.applyConstraints({ advanced: [{ torch: true }] }) API.

Privacy Guarantee

Your camera is used solely to access the physical LED torch. The video stream is never rendered, recorded, stored, or sent over any network.

Platform Compatibility

• Android Chrome / Edge / Opera: Full native hardware torch supported.
• iOS Safari: Apple WebKit does not expose hardware torch control to websites. Use Screen Torch Mode for full brightness white screen.
• Desktop / Laptop: Screen Torch mode simulates flash using monitor brightness.

Hardware Torch Unavailable

Your browser or device does not support direct hardware torch control via WebRTC.

Recommended Solution:

Switch to Screen Torch Mode. It turns your entire display into an ultra-bright white flashlight on touch & hold!
